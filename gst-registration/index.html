Who Needs GST Registration?

GST registration is mandatory in these cases — don't wait for a notice.

Turnover Above ₹20 Lakh

Any business with annual turnover exceeding ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for NE & hill states) must register for GST.

Interstate Supply

Any business supplying goods or services to another state must register for GST, regardless of turnover.

E-Commerce Sellers

All sellers on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, or any other e-commerce platform must register for GST regardless of turnover.

Reverse Charge (RCM)

Businesses required to pay GST under reverse charge mechanism must register even if below the threshold.

Import / Export

Businesses involved in import or export of goods and services must have GSTIN for customs clearance and IGST refunds.

Voluntary Registration

Even if below threshold, voluntary GST registration gives you input tax credit and improved credibility with B2B clients.

Documents Required

Documents vary slightly by entity type — we'll guide you on WhatsApp.

For Companies & LLPs

  • PAN Card of the entity
  • Certificate of Incorporation / LLP Certificate
  • MOA & AOA / LLP Agreement
  • PAN & Aadhaar of directors/partners
  • Bank account details / cancelled cheque
  • Office address proof + NOC (if rented)

For Proprietorships & Individuals

  • PAN Card of the individual
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Bank account details / cancelled cheque
  • Business address proof + NOC (if rented)

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about GST registration in India.

GST registration is mandatory if: annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for NE & hill states); you supply goods or services to another state; you sell on e-commerce platforms like Amazon or Flipkart; you are subject to reverse charge; or you are involved in import/export. Voluntary registration is also possible below the threshold.
The government fee for GST registration is ₹0 — it is completely free on the GST portal. Sakthi Consultancy charges ₹1,499 as professional service fees to handle your application, documents, and follow-up until your GSTIN is issued.
GST registration typically takes 3–7 working days after all documents are submitted. The GST officer may raise a query (called an SCN — Show Cause Notice) requiring clarification on documents, which we handle on your behalf.
GSTIN is your 15-digit GST Identification Number. The first 2 digits are your state code (36 for Telangana, 37 for Andhra Pradesh), followed by your PAN (10 digits), entity number, and check digit. You must print your GSTIN on all GST invoices issued to customers.
Yes. If your business has a fixed establishment (office, warehouse, or place of business) in both Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, you need a separate GSTIN for each state. Sakthi Consultancy handles multi-state GST registrations across all Indian states.
The GST Composition Scheme is a simplified option for businesses with annual turnover below ₹1.5 crore (for goods) or ₹50 lakh (for services). Under this scheme, you pay a fixed GST rate (1–6%) on turnover with reduced compliance — only quarterly filing instead of monthly. You cannot claim input tax credit or issue taxable invoices. Contact us on WhatsApp to check if your business qualifies.
Operating without GST registration when liable is a serious offence. Penalties include: 100% of the tax due or ₹10,000 (whichever is higher) for non-registration; 18% interest on unpaid tax; and potential prosecution under the GST Act. Register immediately if your turnover crosses the threshold.
