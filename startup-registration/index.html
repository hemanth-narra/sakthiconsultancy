Are You Eligible?

All 4 criteria must be met for DPIIT recognition.

Entity Type

Must be incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm. Sole proprietorships and OPCs are not eligible.

Age of Entity

The startup must be less than 10 years old from its date of incorporation or registration.

Turnover Limit

Annual turnover must not have exceeded ₹100 crore in any financial year since incorporation.

Innovation Focus

Must be working towards innovation, development, or improvement of products, processes, or services. Must not be formed by splitting an existing business.

Benefits of DPIIT Recognition

Why every eligible startup in India should get DPIIT recognised.

3-Year Tax Exemption

Apply for income tax exemption under Section 80-IAC for any 3 consecutive years out of first 10 years. Subject to DIPP approval.

Angel Tax Relief

Exemption from Section 56(2)(viib) — investors are not taxed on share premium above fair market value when investing in your startup.

Fast-Track IPR

80% rebate on patent filing fees. Fast-tracked patent examination and dedicated IP facilitation cells for DPIIT startups.

Government Tenders

Exempted from prior experience and turnover requirements when bidding for government tenders — a major advantage for early-stage startups.

Fund of Funds Access

Access to SIDBI's Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS) scheme — ₹10,000 crore committed by the government for startup funding.

Easy Winding Up

DPIIT-recognised startups can wind up their company within 90 days under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Startup India / DPIIT recognition.

DPIIT Recognition is a government certification that grants your startup significant tax exemptions, regulatory relaxations, and funding access. It is issued by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) under the Startup India initiative. Recognition is free — there is no government fee.
To be eligible: your entity must be a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm; less than 10 years old; annual turnover below ₹100 crore; focused on innovation, development, or improvement of a product/service/process. It must not be formed by splitting an existing business.
Key benefits include: 3-year income tax exemption under Section 80-IAC (applies to any 3 out of 10 years); angel tax exemption under Section 56(2)(viib) — investors don't pay tax on share premiums above fair market value; 80% rebate on patent fees; and capital gains exemption when investing in eligible startups.
For most straightforward applications, DPIIT recognition is granted within 2–7 working days after submitting the application on the Startup India portal. Complex applications may take up to 30 days.
