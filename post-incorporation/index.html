GST Registration

Required above ₹20 lakh turnover (₹10 lakh in special states) or for interstate supply. Includes GSTIN, user ID, and first login setup.

Trade License

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about post-incorporation services.

MSME (Udyam) registration is not mandatory but highly beneficial. It enables access to priority-sector lending, collateral-free loans under CGTMSE, government tenders reserved for MSMEs, and subsidies. It's free to register and takes just 1–2 days. We strongly recommend it for all new companies.
Form ADT-1 is the notice of appointment of the first auditor of a company. It must be filed within 15 days of the Board Meeting that appoints the auditor — and that Board Meeting itself must happen within 30 days of incorporation. Missing this deadline incurs penalties. We handle this as part of our Compliance Management package.
Form INC-20A is a Declaration for Commencement of Business, which every company must file within 180 days of incorporation. It confirms that the directors have paid their subscription money into the company's bank account. Failure to file attracts a penalty of ₹50,000 on the company and ₹1,000/day on each defaulting director. We file this for you as part of the Compliance Management pack.
The Compliance Management pack is billed monthly at ₹5,000. There is no minimum lock-in period. You can start or stop at any month. However, we recommend continuous engagement since compliance deadlines are time-sensitive — gaps in management can lead to penalties.
The Bundle is designed for new business owners who want to incorporate their company and get everything set up correctly from day one — without worrying about which registrations they need, what to file, or when. At ₹14,000 one-time, you get the company incorporated + all post-incorporation registrations done + the first month of compliance handled. After month 1, the Compliance Management pack continues at ₹5,000/month if you choose.
