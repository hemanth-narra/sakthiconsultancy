GST Registration in India: Complete Guide for New Businesses (2026)

Sakthi Consultancy Team May 2026 7 min read

GST (Goods and Services Tax) replaced over 17 central and state taxes in India when it was introduced in 2017. For any new business, understanding GST registration — who needs it, when to apply, and what it costs — is essential before you start operating.

This guide is written for business owners in Hyderabad, Vijayawada, and across India who want a clear, practical explanation — not tax jargon.

Who Must Register for GST?

GST registration is mandatory if any of the following apply to your business:

Goods Suppliers

₹40L

Annual turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh (₹20L in special category states)

Service Providers

₹20L

Annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10L in special category states)

You must also register regardless of turnover if:

  • Interstate supply: You sell goods or services to customers in another state
  • E-commerce seller: You sell through Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, or any marketplace
  • Reverse charge mechanism: You receive services from unregistered suppliers above the threshold
  • Casual taxable person: You supply in a state where you don't have a fixed place of business
  • Non-resident taxable person: You supply from outside India
  • Input Service Distributor (ISD)

Telangana (State Code: 36) and Andhra Pradesh (State Code: 37) both follow the standard ₹20L threshold for services. If you operate in both states, you need separate GST registrations for each.

Documents Required for GST Registration

For a Private Limited Company / LLP

  • Certificate of Incorporation
  • PAN card of the company
  • MOA & AOA (for Pvt Ltd) or LLP Agreement
  • Proof of principal place of business (electricity bill / property tax receipt)
  • NOC from owner (if rented premises)
  • PAN and Aadhaar of all directors/designated partners
  • Photograph of the authorised signatory
  • Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement)
  • Board Resolution authorising GST registration

For a Sole Proprietorship

  • PAN card of the proprietor
  • Aadhaar card
  • Proof of business address
  • Bank account details
  • Photograph

Step-by-Step Registration Process

  • 1
    File GST REG-01 on the GST PortalVisit gstin.gov.in. Part A: Enter PAN, email, mobile number. You receive a TRN (Temporary Reference Number). Part B: Fill business details, upload documents, and submit using DSC (for companies) or OTP-based e-signature (for individuals).
  • 2
    Application Reference Number (ARN) IssuedAfter submission, an ARN is generated. This confirms your application is under review. GST officers typically process applications within 7 working days. If no documents are pending, the GSTIN is issued within this period.
  • 3
    GST Officer ReviewThe assigned officer may raise queries or request additional documents via GSTR-REG-03. You must respond within 7 working days via GSTR-REG-04. If documents are in order, approval is issued through GSTR-REG-06.
  • 4
    GSTIN IssuedYour 15-digit GSTIN (GST Identification Number) is issued. The format is: State Code (2 digits) + PAN (10 digits) + Entity Number (2 digits) + Check Digit (1). For Hyderabad: starts with 36. For Vijayawada/AP: starts with 37.
  • 5
    Set Up GST Portal Access & First ReturnLog in with your GSTIN to set up the GST portal account. Your first GSTR-3B return is due by the 20th of the following month after registration. Sakthi Consultancy includes portal setup and first login guidance in our ₹1,499 registration service.

What Happens After GST Registration?

Once registered, you must:

  • Issue GST invoices to all customers with your GSTIN, HSN/SAC code, and applicable tax rate
  • File GSTR-1 monthly (outward supplies) — due 11th of each month
  • File GSTR-3B monthly (summary return + tax payment) — due 20th of each month
  • File GSTR-9 annually (annual return) — due December 31st
  • Maintain GST records for at least 6 years

Penalties for Not Registering

If you are required to register but don't: Penalty of 10% of the tax due (minimum ₹10,000), or 100% of tax due if found to be deliberate evasion. If you collect tax without registration, the penalty is 100% of tax collected. Interest at 18% per annum on unpaid tax also applies.

Voluntary Registration

Even if your turnover is below the threshold, you can voluntarily register for GST. This is beneficial if:

  • Your customers are GST-registered businesses (B2B) — they need your GSTIN to claim input tax credit
  • You want to claim input tax credit on your purchases
  • You are selling online on Amazon/Flipkart (mandatory regardless of turnover)
  • You want to appear more credible to corporate clients
