How to Register a Private Limited Company in India (2026 Complete Guide)

Sakthi Consultancy Team May 2026 8 min read

Registering a Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) in India is the most popular way to start a formal business. It gives you limited liability, a separate legal identity, and the ability to raise funding from investors. The entire process is done online through the MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) portal — and with the right preparation, it takes 7–10 working days.

This guide walks you through every step of the process — from checking name availability to receiving your Certificate of Incorporation — based on our experience incorporating 500+ companies across India from our Hyderabad office.

What is a Private Limited Company?

A Private Limited Company is registered under the Companies Act 2013 and regulated by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). Key features:

  • Minimum 2 directors and 2 shareholders (can be the same people)
  • Maximum 200 shareholders (cannot invite public investment)
  • Limited liability — personal assets are protected from business debts
  • Separate legal entity — the company can own property, sign contracts, and sue/be sued in its own name
  • Can raise equity funding from angel investors, VCs, and private equity

Step-by-Step Registration Process

  • 1
    Obtain Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) for all DirectorsEvery director must have a Class-3 DSC to digitally sign MCA forms. DSC is obtained from MCA-approved certifying authorities. Takes 1–2 working days and costs approximately ₹1,500–₹2,000 per director (government fee, not included in our service fee).
  • 2
    Reserve Your Company Name (SPICe+ Part A)File SPICe+ Part A on the MCA portal to reserve up to 2 proposed names. Names must be unique, not similar to existing companies, and must not include restricted words without approval. MCA typically approves names within 1–3 working days. Tip: Keep names generic and distinctive — avoid generic words like "India" or "Tech" alone.
  • 3
    Apply for Director Identification Number (DIN)DIN is a unique 8-digit number assigned to every company director. For first-time directors, DIN is applied through the SPICe+ form itself (integrated process). No separate application needed for first 3 directors.
  • 4
    File SPICe+ Part B (Main Incorporation Form)This is the core incorporation form. It includes: MOA (Memorandum of Association), AOA (Articles of Association), PAN application, TAN application, ESIC registration, EPFO registration, bank account opening request, and GST registration (optional). All filed as a single integrated form.
  • 5
    MCA Processing & Certificate of IncorporationAfter submission, MCA reviews the application. If documents are in order, the Certificate of Incorporation (COI) is issued within 3–5 working days. The COI includes your CIN (Corporate Identification Number), which is your company's permanent unique identifier.
  • 6
    Receive PAN, TAN & Other RegistrationsPAN and TAN are issued along with the COI (or within 1–2 days separately). Bank account opening, ESIC/EPFO, and GST (if applied) follow within the same week.

Documents Required

For Each Director / Shareholder

  • PAN Card (mandatory)
  • Aadhaar Card or Passport or Voter ID (address proof)
  • Recent bank statement or utility bill (not older than 2 months)
  • Passport-size photograph (white background)
  • Personal email ID and mobile number

For the Registered Office

  • Latest electricity or water bill of the premises
  • NOC (No Objection Certificate) from the property owner — if rented or using a family member's address
  • Rent agreement (if applicable)
  • A home/residential address is perfectly valid as a registered office

You can use your home address as the registered office. Many startups in Hyderabad, Vijayawada, and across India use their founder's home as the initial registered office — it's fully legal.

Timeline

The full registration takes 7–10 working days from the date you submit all documents:

  • Day 1–2: DSC application and approval
  • Day 2–3: Name reservation (SPICe+ Part A)
  • Day 4–7: SPICe+ Part B filing (MOA, AOA, PAN, TAN)
  • Day 8–10: MCA processing & Certificate of Incorporation issued

What to Do After Incorporation

Registration is just the beginning. Within 180 days of incorporation, you must complete these mandatory tasks:

  • INC-20A: Declaration of Commencement of Business — within 180 days (₹50,000 penalty if missed)
  • ADT-1: Appoint your first auditor — within 30 days of incorporation
  • Open a current bank account and deposit the subscribed capital
  • GST Registration — if turnover expected to exceed ₹20 lakh, or for interstate supply
  • MSME Registration — free, highly beneficial for loans and tenders

Private Limited vs Other Structures

Not sure if Pvt Ltd is right for you? Here's a quick guide:

  • Choose Pvt Ltd if: you want to raise funding, have 2+ founders, or plan to scale significantly
  • Choose LLP if: you're a professional service firm (CA, lawyer, consultant) with 2+ partners and don't need investor funding
  • Choose OPC if: you're a solo founder and don't plan to raise equity funding
