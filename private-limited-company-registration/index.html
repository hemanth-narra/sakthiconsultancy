  • At least 1 director must be resident Indian
  • NRIs & foreign nationals can be directors
  • Minimum 2 directors, maximum 15
  • No minimum authorized capital requirement

Why Choose Private Limited Company?

The structure most preferred by investors, banks, and enterprise clients across India.

Limited Liability

Shareholders' personal assets are protected. Liability is limited to the amount invested in the company.

Investor-Ready

The only structure that allows equity funding, Angel investment, and VC participation through share issuance.

Bank & Credit Access

Easier to obtain business loans, overdraft facilities, and open current accounts with major banks.

Credibility

Preferred by government agencies, enterprises, and export clients over proprietorships and partnerships.

Perpetual Existence

The company continues to exist regardless of changes in shareholders or directors.

Separate Legal Entity

The company can own property, enter contracts, and sue or be sued in its own name.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before registering.

A Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) is registered under the Companies Act 2013. It provides limited liability to all shareholders, a separate legal identity, and the ability to issue shares for equity funding. It requires a minimum of 2 directors and 2 shareholders (who can be the same individuals). It is the most preferred business structure for startups and growing businesses across India, Telangana, and Andhra Pradesh.
Sakthi Consultancy charges ₹2,899 as professional service fees. This includes name approval, MOA & AOA drafting, PAN & TAN, and DSC & DIN for 2 directors. Government & MCA fees (stamp duty, DSC charges, MCA filing fees) are charged separately at actuals — these typically range from ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 depending on your state and authorized capital.
The full process takes 7–10 working days from the date of document submission: 1–2 days for DSC, 1–2 days for name reservation, 3–5 days for MCA filing and approval, and 1–2 days for PAN & TAN. Timeline may vary during peak MCA processing periods.

A Private Limited Company must file: Annual Return (MGT-7), Financial Statements (AOC-4), ITR, TDS returns, and conduct at least 4 Board meetings per year. GST filings (if registered). Sakthi Consultancy offers ongoing compliance packages to handle all of this for you.
