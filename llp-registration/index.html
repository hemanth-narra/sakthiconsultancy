Why LLP is Cost-Effective

Lower Compliance Cost

LLPs file only 2 forms annually (Form 8 & Form 11) compared to multiple ROC filings for Private Limited companies — saving on annual compliance fees.

No Dividend Distribution Tax

Profits distributed to LLP partners are not subject to Dividend Distribution Tax (DDT), making it tax-efficient for professional service firms.

Benefits of LLP Registration

Why thousands of professionals and consultants across India choose LLP.

Limited Liability

Each partner's liability is limited to their capital contribution. Personal assets are fully protected.

Minimal Compliance

Only 2 annual forms (Form 8 & Form 11) compared to 4+ ROC filings for a Private Limited Company.

Separate Legal Entity

An LLP can own property, enter contracts, and take legal action in its own name.

Tax Efficient

No Dividend Distribution Tax. Profits distributed to partners are taxed as income — no double taxation.

Perpetual Succession

LLP continues to exist even if a partner leaves. Business operations are unaffected by partner changes.

Flexible Management

Partners can define their roles, profit sharing, and management structure through the LLP Agreement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before registering your LLP.

An LLP combines the flexibility of a partnership with the limited liability protection of a company. All partners' personal assets are protected. It is governed by the LLP Act 2008, registered with MCA, and is a separate legal entity distinct from its partners.
An LLP has lower compliance requirements (only 2 annual forms), no dividend distribution tax, and is ideal for professional services. A Private Limited Company can raise equity funding from investors and has higher credibility with banks. LLPs cannot issue equity shares to investors. If you plan to raise VC/Angel funding, choose Private Limited.
Sakthi Consultancy charges ₹2,899 as professional service fees for LLP registration. This includes name approval, LLP incorporation, LLP agreement drafting, PAN & TAN, and DSC for 2 designated partners. Government filing fees and stamp duty are additional and charged at actuals.
A minimum of 2 designated partners are required. There is no maximum limit on the total number of partners. At least 1 designated partner must be a resident Indian (stayed in India for at least 182 days in the previous year).
An LLP must annually file: Form 8 (Statement of Account & Solvency) by October 30, Form 11 (Annual Return) by May 30, and Income Tax Return (ITR-5). If turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh, a tax audit is required. This is significantly less than a Private Limited Company's compliance load.
