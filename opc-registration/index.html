When to Choose OPC

Solo Business Owner

If you are the only founder and do not need partners or investors, OPC gives you full control with limited liability protection.

Mandatory Conversion

An OPC must convert to a Private Limited Company if paid-up capital exceeds ₹50 lakh or annual turnover exceeds ₹2 crore. We'll guide you through the conversion when the time comes.

Benefits of One Person Company

Why solo entrepreneurs across India choose OPC over sole proprietorship.

Limited Liability

Your personal assets are completely protected. Business debts do not affect your personal finances.

Solo Control

You are the single owner and director. Complete decision-making authority with no interference from partners.

Separate Legal Entity

Your OPC can own property, enter contracts, and operate independently from you as an individual.

Higher Credibility

An OPC has much higher credibility than a sole proprietorship when dealing with clients, banks, and government.

Bank Loans

Easier access to business loans, MSME credit, and government schemes compared to sole proprietorships.

Easy Upgrade

Seamlessly convert to a Private Limited Company when your business grows and needs investors or partners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about OPC registration.

An OPC is a company with a single director and shareholder (the same person), introduced under the Companies Act 2013. It gives solo entrepreneurs the benefits of limited liability and corporate identity without needing a second person or partner.
OPC is ideal for solo founders, freelancers, consultants, designers, and individual business owners across India — in Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, and every other city — who want corporate protection without needing a co-founder.
An OPC must appoint a nominee — an Indian citizen and resident — who will take over the company if the sole director becomes incapacitated or passes away. The nominee must give written consent using Form INC-3 at the time of incorporation. The nominee has no active role while you run the company.
An OPC has only 1 director and 1 shareholder, while a Private Limited Company requires at least 2. OPC cannot raise equity funding from investors. If your OPC's paid-up capital exceeds ₹50 lakh or annual turnover exceeds ₹2 crore, you must convert to a Private Limited Company — we handle this conversion for you.

Yes. An OPC can voluntarily convert to a Private Limited Company at any time after 2 years of incorporation, or mandatorily if turnover exceeds ₹2 crore or paid-up capital exceeds ₹50 lakh. Sakthi Consultancy handles the entire conversion process.
